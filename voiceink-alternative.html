A VoiceInk alternative, with files and 18 languages

VoiceInk is open source, and that is a real advantage this page will not argue away. Here is what VocoSafe AI adds, and when VoiceInk is still the better pick.

Download from Mac App Store

Free to download and free to use. Manual dictation and file transcription have no time limit, and there is no account.

Start here, because it decides most of it. VoiceInk is published under the GPL. You can read every line, build it yourself, and verify what it does with your audio rather than take anyone’s word for it. VocoSafe AI is closed source. If auditability is what you are shopping for, VoiceInk wins and nothing below changes that.

What follows is for people weighing other things: transcribing files, working in a language other than English, and buying through Apple rather than compiling.

Where VoiceInk is the better buy

You want to read the code

For a tool that hears everything you say, “trust me” is a weaker promise than “here is the source”. That is a legitimate reason to pick VoiceInk over VocoSafe AI, and this page will not argue with it.

You want per-app behavior

Power Mode changes how dictation works depending on the app or website in front of you. VocoSafe AI has one mode.

You need custom vocabulary

Names, jargon, drug names, product codes. VoiceInk has a personal dictionary; VocoSafe AI does not have one yet.

You have an Intel Mac, or macOS 14

VoiceInk supports macOS 14. VocoSafe AI needs Apple Silicon and macOS 15, with no workaround.

Where VocoSafe AI is the better buy

You also have recordings to transcribe

VoiceInk is dictation only. VocoSafe AI also transcribes recordings you already have, such as an interview, a lecture or a video, several in one go, with each result saved as its own .txt if you turn that on.

You are not working in English

The whole interface is translated into 18 languages, not just the speech recognition. And SenseVoice is there specifically for Chinese, Japanese and Korean.

You would rather not compile anything

The free VoiceInk route means Xcode and a build. Installing from the Mac App Store is a click, updates arrive on their own, and refunds go through Apple.

You want grammar fixed without a cloud round trip

The punctuation and grammar pass runs on a local Qwen model. Nothing about your text leaves the machine to get cleaned up.

Pricing

VocoSafe AI is free to download and free to use. Manual dictation, audio and video file transcription, history and basic grammar checking stay free with no time limit and no account. PRO adds voice activation and the local AI grammar pass. Monthly and yearly are subscriptions with a one-month free trial; Lifetime is a one-time purchase with no trial. Current prices are on the pricing page.

VoiceInk is free from source and a one-time purchase pre-built, with the price depending on how many Macs you need. Check their site for the current tiers.

Try it before deciding

The free tier is not a trial with a timer. Both apps can be tried for nothing, so the honest advice is to run both for a week.
