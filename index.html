Check it yourself

Privacy you can verify, not just read about.

Every dictation app says it respects your data. Here are three ways to test whether this one means it.

01

Turn off Wi-Fi and keep dictating

Once the model you picked has downloaded, transcription never needs a connection again. Pull the plug mid-sentence and the words still arrive.

02

Watch it with LuLu

Point a firewall at the app and you will see everything it ever asks the network for: the one-time model download, the App Store confirming your purchase, and the license check at launch, which is usually answered from the local cache. What you dictate is not on that list. The audio and the text stay on your Mac, always.

03

No account, no telemetry

Nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to. No analytics, no usage tracking, no crash reporting of your own. No exceptions.

In use

Speak into the window you are already in.

What it does

Dictation that behaves like typing.

Types where you already work

Mail, Pages, a code editor, a chat window. The text lands in whatever has focus, as though you had typed it there yourself.

Cleans up as you speak

Punctuation, capitals and the obvious slips are fixed while you talk, so you are not editing the transcript afterwards.

Pro feature

Starts without touching a key

Say the first word and recording begins. Stop talking and it ends. Nothing to hold down, nothing to click, and it hears when you are done.

Pro feature

Follows you between languages

Speech is auto-detected across more than 90 languages, and the app's own interface is available in 18.

Transcribes recordings too

Drop in an interview, a meeting recording or a video and get the text back, on the same machine, with no upload step. Several files in one go, worked through one after another.

Writes the text out as a file

Turn it on, pick a folder once, and every transcription is saved there as its own .txt, named after the recording it came from. Off until you ask for it, and dictation is never written to disk.

Runs on the Neural Engine

Local models including NVIDIA Parakeet and Whisper Turbo, compiled for Apple Silicon rather than streamed from a data center.

Made for confidential material

Legal drafts, clinical notes, unpublished reporting. Work that cannot go to a third-party server is the reason this app exists.

Who makes this

Built by one developer.

VocoSafe AI is written and supported by Oleksiy Krotov, an independent developer. There is no team, no help desk and no ticket queue: a support request goes to the person who writes the code.

On the Mac, the app is distributed through the Mac App Store. Apple reviews every update for security before it reaches you, and payment is handled securely by Apple. Every version that has shipped is listed in the changelog, with the date it went out.
