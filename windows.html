Speech to text for Windows

VocoSafe AI for Windows is in development.

The on-device engine already runs: the same NVIDIA Parakeet and Whisper Turbo models used on Mac. The Windows app itself has not shipped yet, and no release date is being announced until it is close. This page will be the first place that changes.

Requires Windows 11 on x64.
Note: One-time internet connection required only for AI Models download.

100% Private & Offline

All processing happens locally on your machine. After a one-time download of your chosen AI Models, VocoSafe AI requires zero internet access. No data ever leaves your computer.

Smart AI Post-Processing

Integrated AI automatically fixes grammar, punctuation, and typos in real-time. Experience near-human transcription quality on your own Windows PC.

Seamless System Integration

VocoSafe AI types directly into any active application: Office, Slack, Discord, or your favorite IDE. It works wherever you type.

State-of-the-Art Accuracy

Powered by advanced local models like NVIDIA Parakeet and Whisper Turbo, running on the processor with no dedicated graphics card required.

Multilingual Support

Intelligent auto-detection for English, Spanish, Chinese, Hindi, Arabic, French, German, Japanese, Russian, and 90+ other languages.

Built for Professionals

Perfect for lawyers, doctors, and developers who demand the highest level of data security on their Windows workstations.

Availability and pricing

There is nothing to buy yet. When the Windows app ships, pricing is expected to mirror the Mac app: a monthly plan, a yearly plan and a lifetime purchase, sold through the Microsoft Store. None of that is final until the app is actually available, so treat any number here as a guess rather than a quote.

Stay informed

There is no mailing list yet. The changelog lists every release as it happens, and it will carry the first Windows build the day it goes out.
