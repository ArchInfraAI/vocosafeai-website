A fully offline alternative to superwhisper

superwhisper is the more powerful app, and this page says so plainly. VocoSafe AI is the smaller, cheaper one that never talks to a server at all.

Download from Mac App Store

Free to download and free to use. Manual dictation and file transcription have no time limit, and there is no account.

The short version. superwhisper does more: custom modes that switch by app, cloud models when you want them, an iPhone app, a Windows build. If you want the most capable dictation tool on the Mac and the price is not the issue, buy it.

VocoSafe AI is for the narrower case: you want dictation that has no cloud path to turn on by accident, you would rather not pay a subscription, and you would rather use the app in your own language.

Where superwhisper is the better buy

You want different behavior per app

A mode for email, another for Slack, another for code, switching by itself depending on what is in front of you. VocoSafe AI has one mode and one shortcut. This is superwhisper’s strongest feature, and VocoSafe AI has no answer to it.

You need more than a Mac

iPhone, iPad and Windows on the same license. VocoSafe AI is macOS only today; the Windows version is being worked on and there is no iOS app.

You have an Intel Mac

VocoSafe AI needs Apple Silicon and there is no workaround. The models are built for the neural engine.

You want cloud models when it suits you

Sometimes a cloud model is simply more accurate, or you want an LLM in the loop. VocoSafe AI cannot do that, deliberately.

Where VocoSafe AI is the better buy

“Offline” has to mean there is no other option

An app that can use the cloud has a setting that can be switched on, by you, by an update default, or by someone else using your Mac. VocoSafe AI has no such setting because it has no server. Block it in a firewall like LuLu and it keeps working.

You are not working in English

The whole interface is translated into 18 languages, not just the speech recognition. Every alternative on this page ships an English-only interface.

The price matters

superwhisper is priced as a professional tool and is worth it to professionals. VocoSafe AI costs a fraction of that, and its free tier is genuinely unlimited for dictation and file transcription. Compare the current figures yourself.

You would rather buy through Apple

Bought on the Mac App Store: refunds handled by Apple, family sharing, a sandbox the OS enforces, and a review before every update. Some people care about that a great deal; if you do not, it is not an argument.

Pricing

VocoSafe AI is free to download and free to use. Manual dictation, audio and video file transcription, history and basic grammar checking stay free with no time limit and no account. PRO adds voice activation and the local AI grammar pass. Monthly and yearly are subscriptions with a one-month free trial; Lifetime is a one-time purchase with no trial. Current prices are on the pricing page.

superwhisper’s prices have changed more than once and differ by plan, so rather than quote a number that may already be stale, check their site.

Try it before deciding

The free tier is not a trial with a timer. Dictate with it for a week and see whether the extra features you would be paying for elsewhere are ones you use.
